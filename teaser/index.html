The moat

01

Data that compounds

Every order deepens the guest graph — preferences, usuals, win-back triggers. A competitor lands on day one with an empty database; the AI that already knows the guest sells more from the first ring.

02

Switching costs, multiplied

Phone, website, app, and loyalty run on one contract and one guest layer. Leaving Bytes means ripping out four systems at once — and abandoning the guest history that powers all of them.
